01 · About

Simulation engineer at the intersection of physics, software & autonomy.

I'm a Machine Simulation Engineer at Caterpillar, where I build simulations that let engineering teams develop and validate machine control and autonomy software before a physical machine is ever available. My work spans the full stack: from converting high-fidelity physics models into real-time plant models, to integrating virtual and physical controllers on HIL/SIL benches, to writing C++ sensor simulators for the autonomy stack.

Today I lead the development of GPU-accelerated Physical AI simulation on NVIDIA Newton and Isaac Sim, where hundreds of haul trucks run in parallel to train and test autonomy, and I'm building agentic AI pipelines that turn engineering data into sim-ready machine models in days instead of months.

I hold a Master's in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics) from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
